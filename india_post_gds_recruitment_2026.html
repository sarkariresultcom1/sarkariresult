India Post GDS Recruitment 2026 FAQs

Q1. What is the total number of vacancies in India Post GDS 2026?

There are a massive total of 23,757 vacancies available across India for the Gramin Dak Sevak (GDS) Schedule II July 2026 recruitment cycle.

Q2. What is the minimum qualification required for India Post GDS?

Candidates must have passed Class 10th (High School) from a recognized board with Mathematics and English as compulsory subjects. Additionally, knowledge of the local language of the applied postal circle is mandatory.

Q3. What is the last date to apply for GDS 2026?

The online registration ends on 19 September 2026, and the last date to complete the application form and pay the exam fee is 21 September 2026 (up to 5:00 PM).

Q4. What is the age limit for Gramin Dak Sevak?

The minimum age to apply is 18 years, and the maximum age is 40 years. Reserved category candidates (SC/ST/OBC/PH) will receive age relaxation as per government rules.
